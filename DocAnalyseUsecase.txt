Legal contract analysis and summarization are valuable tasks, but there are several other functions you can perform with AI in the context of legal contracts:

1. *Contract Review and Analysis*: AI can assist in reviewing contracts for potential risks, inconsistencies, or clauses that might need attention. It can identify key terms, obligations, and deadlines, helping legal professionals to focus their attention on critical areas.

2. *Contract Comparison*: AI can compare multiple contracts to identify similarities, differences, and deviations from standard language or terms. This can streamline the process of creating new contracts or negotiating terms.

3. *Contract Generation*: AI can assist in generating contracts based on predefined templates and user inputs. This can save time for legal professionals by automating the drafting process for routine contracts.

4. *Clause Extraction*: AI can extract specific clauses or sections from contracts based on user-defined criteria. This can be useful for due diligence, compliance checks, or research purposes.

5. *Contract Risk Assessment*: AI can assess the risk level associated with specific clauses or provisions within a contract. It can identify potential legal or financial risks and provide recommendations for mitigation.

6. *Natural Language Understanding (NLU)*: AI can answer questions and provide explanations based on the content of legal contracts. This can help users understand complex legal language and interpret the meaning of specific clauses.

7. *Contract Lifecycle Management (CLM)*: AI can automate various stages of the contract lifecycle, including drafting, negotiation, execution, and management. This can improve efficiency and reduce the risk of errors or oversights.

8. *Compliance Monitoring*: AI can monitor contracts for compliance with regulatory requirements, internal policies, or industry standards. It can flag potential issues or deviations from compliance standards and alert relevant stakeholders.

9. *Predictive Analytics*: AI can analyze past contract data to identify patterns, trends, and insights that can inform future decision-making. This can help organizations optimize contract terms, pricing strategies, and risk management approaches.

10. *Document Understanding*: AI can extract structured data from unstructured legal documents, such as contracts, using techniques like natural language processing (NLP) and optical character recognition (OCR). This data can then be used for various analytical purposes.
